# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Software engineers reading practical explanations of architecture, infrastructure, and reliable systems.

## Product Purpose

Mouaad Aallam's personal website shares engineering knowledge. Success means helping readers understand technical concepts and apply them in their work. The author's profile supports the writing.

## Operating Context

The current site uses Jekyll, Markdown posts, Liquid templates, and Sass. GitHub Actions builds and deploys it to GitHub Pages at https://mouaad.aallam.com.

Readers discover articles through the blog, tags, related posts, and the feed, and can learn about the author through the About page and profile links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing article URLs and their availability through direct links.
- Preserve archived and unlisted visibility rules across the blog, tags, related posts, feed, and sitemap, including existing indexing directives.
- Use real authored content and assets; do not invent claims or supporting evidence.
- Keep explanations plain and technically accurate.
- Jekyll is the current implementation, not a permanent requirement. A future stack change is allowed; its timing and replacement are undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

- Authored engineering articles and examples in `_posts/`.
- Author information and contact links in `about.md` and `_config.yml`.
- Profile image at `assets/images/profile.png`, social card at `assets/images/social-card.png`, and existing illustrations in `assets/images/`.

## Product Principles

1. Put technical accuracy and useful engineering knowledge first.
2. Explain concepts in plain language that readers can apply.
3. Preserve published links and deliberate content visibility choices as the site evolves.
